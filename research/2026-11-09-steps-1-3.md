# GMC Suspension Checker + Outcome Log: Steps 1–3 (research only, no code)

## Context
Long chose a bet on 2026-11-09: a Shopify app that checks a store against Google Merchant Center (GMC) misrepresentation criteria and logs fix → reinstatement outcomes to build a data moat (hào phòng thủ). This document covers steps 1–3: re-verify the evidence, a competitor gap table, and a one-page bet. Every number carries a date and a source. Steps 4–7 and all code wait for Long's "go".

**Short answer:** the bet survives, but its wedge (mũi nhọn) must change. A plain checker is now a commodity (hàng phổ thông): 4 new Shopify apps have launched since June 2026, 3 of them on Oct 6–14. Public fix → outcome data is much thinner than assumed. Details below.

### 1c. Google-side facts that matter
- **Review cost** ([Request a review](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13585221), read 2026-11-09): "Wait 7 business days", and a review "may take up to 2 weeks". After the 2nd unsuccessful attempt "a one-week cool down period may begin" and it "may increase with each unsuccessful review". Support can't shorten it. Misrepresentation suspensions may also need merchant verification (government ID plus business documents). → **Every wasted review costs 1–3+ weeks.** This is the sharpest honest pitch we have.
- **Policy consolidation:** Google announced on 2026-07-15 ([change log](https://support.google.com/merchants/announcements/6192467), [ppc.land 2026-07-20](https://ppc.land/google-merges-two-shopping-policy-sets-but-adds-zero-merchant-restrictions/)) that Shopping ads and free listings policies merge into one set in Sept 2026, with "no substantive changes". The new Misrepresentation page is `answer/17234184`, linked from the [new policy hub](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/17250716); the old [6150127](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127) still loads. → **Step 4 must link to the new IDs.**
- **Suspension counts:** Google's 2025 Ads Safety Report (released 2026-04-16, [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/googles-2025-ads-safety-report-gemini-blocked-8-3-billion-bad-ads/)) gives 24.9M *advertiser* accounts suspended (39.2M in 2024) and says incorrect suspensions fell 80%. Those are Google **Ads** figures. Google publishes **no** Merchant Center suspension count. StubGroup's "5–10M GMC suspensions in 2025" ([source](https://stubgroup.com/blog/the-state-of-google-ads-suspensions-2025/)) is an unofficial vendor estimate, so **never use it in marketing.**
- **AI surfaces:** On 2026-05-27 Google added insights on products found through AI Mode, AI Overviews and the Gemini app, using Merchant Center data ([change log](https://support.google.com/merchants/announcements/6192467)). My inference, not a Google statement: a suspension now costs more than Shopping ads.
- **Retracted claim, do not use:** "Google added AI verification of stores in April 2026". The author withdrew it on 2026-11-06 ([dev.to](https://dev.to/aukedh/google-merchant-center-ai-verification-2026-what-actually-changed-in-april-266p)).

### 1d. Public fix → outcome data is scarce (affects the moat)
- [Shopify Community checklist thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/checklist-suspended-google-merchant-center-account-fix-resolve/38317), page 1 (19 replies, 2021-03 → 2021-10): **0 replies report a reinstatement together with a named fix**, and 1 reports a failed appeal.
- ["Suspended despite full compliance"](https://community.shopify.com/t/google-merchant-center-suspended-for-misrepresentation-despite-full-compliance/575435) (2025-11-13 → 2026-09-07): the merchant fixed policies, identity, feed attributes, HTTPS and 404s, appealed several times, and is still suspended. No outcome is reported.
- Reddit (via mirrors, 2026-03 / 2026-05): mostly unresolved posts.
- → Seeding the outcome log from public posts will yield **tens of pairs, not hundreds**. Step 5 has to plan around that.

### What changed the bet
1. **The checker is now a commodity.** The 8 Shopify apps and 2 web tools converge on the same bundle: scan, fix steps, AI policy writer, appeal letter, "emergency pass". A 2-weekend checker would be app #9 with nothing new.
2. **Low traction is a warning, not only an opening.** 11 competitor reviews in total, plus ~1.3 relevant complaints a month on Google's app. Either distribution is hard or the paying segment is small. The step 6 test has to tell these apart.
3. **The moat is slower than assumed.** Public pairs are rare, so most data must come from our own users.
4. **The cooldown rules help us.** "Don't waste a review attempt" is a concrete, honest promise with no prediction in it.

**Fix to the bet (my recommendation):** don't ask the merchant "which fixes did you make?". People forget and over-report, which is self-report bias (sai lệch do tự khai báo). Instead, **re-scan when they click "I'm requesting review now" and record which checks flipped from fail to pass.** The fixes then come from a diff. The only question left is "Reinstated? yes / no / still waiting", which takes one tap. The merchant gets a dated "what we changed" record for their appeal on day 1, and we get clean data.

> 🇻🇳 Tóm tắt: Bằng chứng gốc vẫn đúng, nhưng thị trường "checker" đã bão hòa (8 app, 11 review tổng) và dữ liệu công khai về kết quả rất ít, nên phải tự động ghi lại thay đổi (diff) thay vì hỏi người dùng.

Others: Torhen ($24.99, readiness gate), ShopSniff ($29 / $99 one-time), GMC Compliance Checker ($19.99, product-text claims only), AdNabu (free, 22 checks).

**The complaints we can actually see (only a few exist):**
1. Paywall before any value (ComplianceGuard 1★; GMC Scout hides details).
2. False positives and fix steps that aren't Shopify-specific (ShieldKit 2★).
3. From Google's app and the forums: "I fixed everything, I'm still suspended, and nobody tells me what to try next" (ZalLux 2026-08-27; Linda's Look 2026-07-31; thread 575435).

**The ONE flaw none of them fix well: they all stop at the review request.** Each tool ranks its findings by its own guessed severity and then goes silent. None of them records what the merchant changed, whether Google reinstated them, or what to try on attempt #2. So the merchant who already fixed everything and got rejected gets the same green report again. That is the most desperate merchant and the one most willing to pay. Torhen's "readiness gate" and Merchant Guard's score are guesses presented as readiness, and they come close to the "prediction" claim we must never make.

Honesty limit: we can't claim "evidence-ranked" until the data exists. Until then, every finding is labeled "Google's help text says X", with a link, and never "this works".

> 🇻🇳 Tóm tắt: Mọi đối thủ đều dừng ở bước "gửi yêu cầu xem xét", không ai ghi lại đã sửa gì và kết quả ra sao; đó là lỗ hổng chung.

## Step 3: One-page bet

**Problem.** Google suspends a Shopify store's Merchant Center account for "misrepresentation" with a generic notice. Each review request costs 7 business days to 2 weeks. After the 2nd failed attempt, a cooldown (thời gian chờ phạt) starts and grows with every failure ([Google, read 2026-11-09](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13585221)). Merchants guess, change 10 things at once, fail, and don't know what to try next.

**Customer.** A small Shopify merchant (1–10 people; US, UK, AU or CA) who sells through the Google & YouTube app and is **currently suspended or warned for misrepresentation**. *Not* sellers of prohibited products (e.g. live animals): no storefront fix helps them, so screen them out early. Secondary customer: agencies and freelancers who manage GMC for clients. GMC Scout already sells white-label PDFs to them.

**Why now.**
1. The category is forming right now. 8 apps launched in about 11 months and the best has 6 reviews (2026-11-09). Search rankings and reviews aren't locked in yet. This is also the warning from Step 1.
2. Merchant Center now feeds AI Mode and Gemini shopping (2026-05-27), so being suspended costs more (inference).
3. Google's consolidated Shopping policies (Sept 2026) give one stable set of URLs to map checks to.

**Wedge: "Don't waste a review."**
- A free **full** scan: every finding is visible with no paywall. This attacks ComplianceGuard's and GMC Scout's weak spot.
- Each finding gives (a) the exact line of Google's help doc with a link and (b) the exact Shopify admin path to fix it (e.g. *Settings → Policies → Refund policy*). This attacks ShieldKit's complaint.
- **Pre-review snapshot:** a button reads "I'm about to request a review". It re-scans and shows *exactly what changed since the first scan*, with dates. The merchant can paste that list into the appeal. No competitor does this.
- **Build now.**

**Moat: the outcome log.**
- 10–14 days after the snapshot, an in-app banner asks: "Reinstated? Yes / No / Still waiting".
- The fixes come from the scan diff, not from memory.
- Stored only with explicit opt-in (đồng ý chủ động): suspension label, IDs of checks that flipped fail → pass, attempt number, days between attempts, outcome. No shop URL in the shared set and no customer data.
- The later ranking shows "of N merchants who fixed X before the review, M were reinstated", always with N displayed.
- **Build now.**

**Moat math (pushback on how fast this happens):** assume 200 installs/month, 40% actually suspended, 50% of those request a review within 30 days, and 40% of those answer. That is about 16 outcomes a month. For one check to reach n ≥ 30 "fixed" cases takes months, and a ranking *per suspension type* takes 6–12 months. Merchants also fix several things at once, so tying an outcome to one fix (quy kết nguyên nhân) is weak until N is large. **v1 must sell on the wedge alone.** The moat is a slow bonus, not the launch pitch. Step 5 will set the exact thresholds.

**Pricing** (market on 2026-11-09: $19.99–$49.99/mo subscriptions; one-time $99 for 30 days at ShopSniff, $149 for 7 days at ComplianceGuard). Getting reinstated is a one-time job (theo đợt), and a subscription churns the day the merchant is reinstated.
- **Free:** full scan with every finding and fix shown, plus 1 snapshot. *Build now.*
- **Reinstatement Pass: $39 one-time, 60 days.** Unlimited re-scans, attempt tracker with diffs, "what we changed" appeal summary, outcome history. A merchant suspended for 2 months pays ClearCheck $55.98 (2 × $27.99); we're cheaper, match the job, and sit well under the $99 / $149 emergency anchors. *Sell after DSO check.*
- **Later, not in v1:** "Monitor" at $9/mo after reinstatement. *Sell after DSO check.*
- The step 6 waitlist page shows $39, so that is the price we test.

**Riskiest assumption.** Merchants come back 1–3 weeks later and answer the outcome question at a useful rate: **≥30% of those who took a snapshot.** Without that there is no moat, and this is checker #9. The second risk is distribution: can a 0-review app get found among 8 others? Step 6 tests distribution. The cheapest pre-launch proxy for the outcome risk: in the free web checker, offer "Email me in 14 days so I can log whether I got reinstated" and measure the opt-in rate.

**Claims we will never make:** "readiness score", "approval chance", "pass = reinstated". Allowed: "18 of 20 published Google criteria checked", plus "X of N merchants who fixed this were reinstated (self-reported)".

> 🇻🇳 Tóm tắt: Mũi nhọn là "đừng phí một lần xin xem xét" (scan miễn phí + snapshot diff), hào phòng thủ là dữ liệu kết quả nhưng sẽ chậm 6–12 tháng; giá đề xuất $39 trả một lần, chỉ thu tiền sau khi DSO xác nhận.
